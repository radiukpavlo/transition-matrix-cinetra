# Protocol amendments log

Every change to a value frozen by the `protocol-v6.0.0` tag (SPECIFICATION.md 6.0.0, `registry/`, `configs/`) requires a dated entry here, a new tag `protocol-v6.0.x`, and a statement of whether any held-out or external partition had already been unblinded when the change was made (which makes the change post hoc).

The 1 October 2026 entry records a design and implementation-plan amendment. It does not activate changed frozen values: the neural specification/configuration/implementation must be reconciled and tagged together before reported neural development. The design JSON under `protocol/` is not a runtime configuration. No signature, freeze or unexposed status is asserted by this entry.

The two 2 October 2026 entries are data-governance decisions: cloud-processing exception CPE-1 and cloud-storage exception CSE-1. They change no file or value frozen by `protocol-v6.0.0`, so they need no `protocol-v6.0.x` tag; v6 execution remains local-only.

The 3 October 2026 entry is the `protocol-v6.0.1` amendment. It is pending the owner's signature. Nothing in it is a signature, an approval, a freeze or a result. Its values bind only when the owner signs the annotated tag `protocol-v6.0.1` on the amendment commit.

**Decisions and deviations recorded on 3 October 2026 for the `protocol-v6.0.1` entry, before signing.** Each is also stated in SPECIFICATION.md 6.0.1 or in the cited documents.

1. **D-1 (owner):** the second confirmatory family H4 (Decision D36). H4a: ρ_EF transfers. H4b: label-free harmonization H-a improves ρ. It is tested on `mms2_external` with Bonferroni-adjusted two-sided 97.5 % intervals, and is descriptive on the historical partitions.
2. **D-2 (owner):** the distinct values of DISEASE, VENDOR, SCANNER and FIELD were read before the tag. No subject codes or counts were read, and the read is logged in `<local path>`.
   - The release spells dilated right ventricle `RV`. It is now a declared synonym of DRV and stays in the RV family.
   - Separator-only blank rows are dropped. A subject row with an empty or unknown value still fails closed.
   - The CSV header and the member names were read on 2 October with the owner's approval.
3. **O1 and O3 (owner-approved):** D4 symmetrization of F9 (one dihedral draw per patient in training; 8-transform averaging of logits and A at inference) and of D20 (8-transform feature averaging).
   - **Reason:** after canonical orientation, about 40 % of M&Ms-1 slices are vertically mirrored relative to ACDC. B9 measures this; the v6 descriptors are orientation-invariant.
   - **Deviation:** for the F9 comparator only, this departs from NEURAL-TM-1.0 4.2 ("no reflections; deterministic evaluation transform").
   - **D20 choices** (canonical in-plane orientation; torchvision bilinear resize with antialias) are stated in SPECIFICATION 8.4.
   - **B9** joins the exploratory register.
4. **ECE (owner):** equal-mass ECE with 10 bins (the v6.0.0 metric) is primary for every predictor; equal-width ECE is reported beside it as a companion.
5. **F9 technical-validity gate:** a pre-evaluation bug check in `freeze-f9`, never a tuning step. It requires development OOF macro-F1 ≥ 0.30, a falling training loss in every refit, and nonzero weight change.
6. **Reader study:**

   - The SAP v2 addendum (R2–R4, `analyze-reader-addendum`) is frozen by hash before import.
